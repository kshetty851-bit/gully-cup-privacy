Gully Cricket Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Gully Cricket ("the game") is a cricket game for Android. This policy explains what information the game uses.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or location.

Information stored on your phone

The game saves your settings, match wins, Gully Cup progress and a timing calibration on your own device so it can remember them next time. This stays on your phone, is never sent to us, and is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Google Play Games

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the game also keeps a copy of your settings, wins and Gully Cup progress in your Google Play Games saved games, so you can continue on another phone. This copy is stored by Google under your Play Games account, not by us, as described in Google's Privacy Policy. You can delete it from the Play Games app.

Purchases

The Gully Cup tournaments, special bats and packs can be unlocked with one-time in-app purchases. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play: we never see your card, UPI or bank details. Google tells the game which Cups, bats and packs your Google account has unlocked so it can open them, also after a reinstall. See Google Payments Privacy Notice.

Advertising

The game can show short video ads from Google AdMob, but only when you choose to watch one: the game always asks first and tells you what you get (one more chance after losing, a free Gully Cup ticket once a day, or a daily challenge counted twice). If you say no, nothing is shown. There are no ads you did not ask for.

The ads are never personalised. Every ad request is marked as child-directed and limited to content suitable for a general audience, and the game does not use your device's advertising ID. To show an ad, measure it and prevent fraud, Google AdMob may receive technical information such as your IP address, device type and model, app version and how you interacted with the ad. We do not receive this information. In the European Economic Area and the UK you will be asked for consent first where the law requires it. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
